# Security

Report a vulnerability privately through the repository's **Security > Report a vulnerability** page, not as a public issue. Include the version, the OS and the steps to reproduce.

You get an answer within a week. Fixes ship as a patch release, and the reporter is credited in its notes unless they ask otherwise.

App runs on your machine and only connects to the network to download the files you choose and to check for updates. Updates are verified against the release's SHA-256 checksums before they install.
